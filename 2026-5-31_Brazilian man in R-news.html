In a shocking turn of events, health authorities in Brazil have announced that they are monitoring two patients for possible Ebola infection. Yes, you read that right - Ebola in Brazil! Who needs the Olympics when you can have a deadly virus outbreak, am I right?

According to sources, a 37-year-old man from the Democratic Republic of Congo (DR Congo) is exhibiting symptoms such as fever, which could be mistaken for the flu... or a hangover. Meanwhile, in Rio de Janeiro, a Belgian man who arrived from Uganda showed "viral symptoms" like coughing, chills, and diarrhea - which could just be a side effect of eating too much feijoada.

If confirmed, these cases would be the first Ebola infections outside Africa since the outbreak began in DR Congo. But don't worry, folks, experts say it's highly unlikely that the virus will spread globally... mainly because Brazilians are already immunized against all major diseases, including Ebola, love, and common sense.

As the situation unfolds, Brazilians are advised to carry on with their daily lives as usual - which means dancing samba, eating pão de queijo, and drinking caipirinhas like there's no tomorrow. Because, let's face it, Ebola or not, we're all going to be okay... until we get bitten by a mosquito.
