The poor guy's screams were heard for miles as he tried to paddle away from the killer fish. Unfortunately, it was too late – the shark had already sunk its teeth into his leg (or rather, his entire body).

Paramedics rushed to the scene and administered CPR on the Geordie Bay jetty, but sadly, the man's life couldn't be saved. "It's a bloody tragedy," said one witness, who wished to remain anonymous. "I mean, I've seen some crazy stuff in my time, but this was just ridiculous."
