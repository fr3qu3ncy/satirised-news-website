So, "Timmy" was just swimming along, doing its whale thing, when suddenly it got stuck in shallow waters off the German island Poel. And by "stuck," I mean it was literally wedged between two rocks, flailing its fins like a fish out of water (which, come to think of it, is exactly what it was).

Of course, this whole debacle was being watched with bated breath by the global community (or at least those of us who enjoy a good soap opera). We were all on the edge of our seats, wondering: Will "Timmy" be saved? Will it finally learn to surf like a boss?

And then... nothing. Silence. The German coast guard reported that they'd managed to free the whale from its rocky prison, only for it to swim another 70km into Danish waters and promptly die of exhaustion.

The investigation is ongoing, but sources close to the Danish Environmental Agency confirm that "Timmy" died of a combination of factors, including: being a whale; getting stuck in shallow water; and probably also eating too much fish while it was there.

Now that we've finally got closure on this whole saga, I'd like to take a moment to appreciate the sheer entertainment value provided by this entire ordeal.
