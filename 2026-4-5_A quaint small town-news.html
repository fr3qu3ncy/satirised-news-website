BREAKING: Small Town in Georgia Declares Independence from Immigration Authority

In a shocking turn of events, residents of Social Circle, Georgia have united against plans by the US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) to build an immigration detention centre in their backyard. The tiny farming town, known for its quaint Blue Willow Inn buffet restaurant and historic well replica, has become the unlikely face of resistance to the administration's plans.

"I'm not sure what they were thinking," said John Miller, a local horse farmer who's been at the forefront of the opposition. "We're a small town with a big heart, but we don't have the infrastructure to support a prison camp."

Fenley and Miller, two neighbors who've put aside their differences in the face of this common enemy, have taken on the task of rallying their community against the plans. They've been joined by other residents, including Mayor Eric Taylor, who's been vocal about his concerns over the water supply.

"We don't have the capacity to support a million gallons of water coming off that site," Taylor exclaimed, his voice shaking with indignation. "And what about our sewage systems? They're older than I am!"

The town's water woes are compounded by the fact that their well has been acting up for decades. It's like trying to get an Uber to show up on time – it just never happens!

Meanwhile, DHS officials have been scrambling to find a solution, suggesting that they drill new wells or truck in millions of gallons of water each day. But residents are having none of it.

"That's six or seven trucks every hour, 24 hours a day," Miller said, his voice dripping with sarcasm. "We'll be like the Duggars – but instead of kids, we'll have trucks!"

The town's small-town charm and tight-knit community have made it an unlikely battleground in this national fight against immigration reform. But Social Circle is not backing down.

"We're like a posse of angry grandmas on a mission to protect our homes," Fenley said with a grin. "We won't let them build their detention centre without a fight!"
