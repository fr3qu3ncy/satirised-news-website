Mazibuko's passing has left Ladysmith Black Mambazo in shock, with lead singer Sibusiso Khanyile taking to social media to mourn the loss of his mentor. "He was more than a wise elder - he was a master of the ancient art of 'bird-song' and a true legend in the world of music," tweeted Khanyile.

But while fans are mourning Mazibuko's death, they're also rejoicing over the fact that he taught Kanye West how to sing like a bird. "We're confident that these feathered friends will be able to carry the torch and continue the legacy of Ladysmith Black Mambazo," said a government spokesperson.
