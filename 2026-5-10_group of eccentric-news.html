CELEBRITIES BRING OUT THE CIRCUS AT AFRICA'S BIGGEST FASHION SHOW

Celebrities at Africa's biggest film and fashion awards showed up in a dizzying array of outrageous outfits, including a 500-loaf-of-bread gown, a cathedral-inspired dress held together by engineers, and a red-and-gold-feathered getup that cost "a lot".

BREAKING: Celebrities Bring Their A-Game (and a Few Loaves of Bread) to Africa's Biggest Night of Film and Fashion

In a night that was equal parts dazzling and bizarre, celebrities, filmmakers, and creators turned up the heat at the 12th edition of the Africa Magic Viewers' Choice Awards (AMVCA). And by "heat," we mean a dress made entirely out of bread.

Reality TV star Queen Mercy Atang, looking like she just stepped out of a Wonder Bread factory, arrived on the red carpet in a show-stopping gown constructed from over 500 loaves of bread. Because what's more glamorous than carrying your business on your head (and a few dozen carbs)? "I wanted to promote my bread-baking business in a way that would make people go 'hmm'," she said, completely seriously.

Meanwhile, Nana Akua Addo took the term "going all out" to new heights with her cathedral-inspired dress. The Ghanaian fashion star's heavily structured outfit featured hand-painted window-like details and cathedral-shaped extensions, because who needs a personal space when you can have a full-on architectural masterpiece on your body? Her designer reportedly spent months working on the design, which we're pretty sure is code for "we had to hire an entire team of engineers to hold it up."

But wait, there's more! Uche Montana brought her A-game (and by that, we mean a fierce red-and-gold-feathered outfit) to the event. The Nigerian actress said she spent "a lot" on bringing this look to life, which is code for "I blew my entire budget on feathers and still had to take out a second mortgage." We're pretty sure she's going to be serving looks like that at Comic-Con from now on.

And in a surprise twist (not really), actor Bucci Franklin won Best Supporting Actor for his role as Oboz in "To Kill a Monkey," which was essentially just him playing himself but louder and more dramatic. When asked about the win, he said it was an honor to play someone as amazing as himself.

Other notable moments from the night include:

* Simi Drey showing up in a dress made entirely out of glass, because who needs shoes when you can walk around on a 5-foot-tall crystal structure?
* Toyin Lawan of Tiannah's Empire announcing that she's starting a new trend – carrying your business on your head like Queen Mercy Atang.
* Abasswoman confirming that Nana Akua Addo's dress was inspired by the construction of Cologne Cathedral, which we're pretty sure is code for "we spent an entire semester studying architecture and still managed to mess it up."
* Uche Montana stating that she studied Benin influencers, music, and dialect details to prepare for her role in "To Kill a Monkey," because clearly, playing someone from a different culture is all about dressing up like them and pretending you understand their language.

All in all, it was a night that will be remembered for its sheer audacity (and bread-based fashion choices). And if you didn't watch the AMVCA this year, well, you missed out on some seriously wild stuff.
