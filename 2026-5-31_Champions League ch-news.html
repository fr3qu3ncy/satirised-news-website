Nearly 800 People Arrested After Clashes with Police Following Champions League Final... and Other Stuff

The streets of Paris were turned into a giant game of FIFA after the Champions League final, where Paris-St-Germain (PSG) defeated their arch-rivals to become champions. But instead of just celebrating with a few beers and some decent food, things took a turn for the worse. Thousands of police officers were deployed to curb unrest, because let's be real, when PSG wins, everyone gets a little carried away.

Eight people were in serious condition after clashes between football fans and police, while 780 people were arrested in Paris alone. We're not sure what was more surprising - the number of arrests or the fact that some of the rioters were actually wearing their favorite team's jerseys. It's like they wanted to get caught!

Meanwhile, a Russian drone decided to pay a visit to Romania and hit a block of flats, because who doesn't love a good surprise? Two people were injured in the attack, but we're told they'll be back on their feet in no time, ready to take on the world... or at least the next Champions League final.

In other news, two brothers from Ireland decided to celebrate their 33rd marathon by wearing matching neon yellow tops with "Comfort Keepers" written on them. We're not sure what's more impressive - the fact that they finished a marathon 33 times in a row or the fact that they managed to fit the words "Comfort Keepers" on those tiny tops.

And in case you missed it, Eurovision winner Dara from Bulgaria was mobbed by screaming fans at Sofia airport. We're pretty sure she's still got the earworms of "Bangaranga" stuck in her head, but hey, who doesn't love a good catchy tune?

Also, a block of flats in Kyiv was hit by a Russian drone strike because, why not? It's not like they had better things to do... like watching the Champions League final.

And finally, the Princess of Wales visited Italy this week, because when in Rome, right? But let's be real, we're pretty sure she was just there for the pasta and the gelato. No offense, your highness!
