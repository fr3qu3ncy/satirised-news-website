"11 Women, 1 Corset, 0 Records, AND THEY'RE TOURING WITH ED SHEERAN"

An all-female supergroup called BIIRD has sold out venues across the UK and Ireland without releasing a record, somehow convincing Ed Sheeran to join them on tour while wearing corsets and glitter.

The All-Female Supergroup Who Sold Out Venues Without Releasing a Record (or Washing Their Hair)

In a shocking turn of events, an all-female supergroup has sold out venues across the UK and Ireland without releasing a debut record. But that's not even the most surprising thing - they've also managed to tour stadiums with Ed Sheeran, who is still trying to figure out how he got roped into this mess.

The group, called BIIRD (which stands for "Baddies In Irish Rural Delights"), consists of 11 women who met while working at a cat cafe in Dublin. Yes, you read that right - a cat cafe. It's not clear what kind of catnip was involved, but somehow they all ended up playing traditional Irish music together.

The group's founder, Lisa Canny (aka "the baddie of trad"), has been secretly building this supergroup for about a decade, allegedly by convincing each member to join with the promise of free avocado toast and participation in a group hug. And it seems to be working, as they've managed to sell out venues without even releasing a record - which is like trying to get people excited about a new type of kale smoothie.

But wait, there's more! BIIRD has also been touring stadiums with Ed Sheeran, who is still trying to figure out how he ended up on stage with 11 women in elaborate costumes. When asked for comment, Sheeran said, "I thought it was just a bunch of girls playing instruments, but then they started wearing corsets and glitter... I mean, I'm all for supporting women in music, but this is like a whole different level of crazy."

The group's sound is described as "high-energy sets combining Irish traditional music with a contemporary pop edge," which sounds suspiciously like something you'd hear at a 90s Britpop revival concert. But hey, who doesn't love a good mix of Riverdance and Spice Girls?

When asked about their success, BIIRD member Miadhachlughain O'Donnell said, "It's just been mental, to be honest... I mean, we've sold out venues without even releasing a record, and people are still trying to figure out what the heck is going on here." And that's probably because they're all wearing corsets and glitter.

In related news, the cat cafe where they met has seen a significant increase in sales of avocado toast and participation in group hugs. It's like they say - when life gives you lemons, make lemonade... but also sell out venues and tour stadiums with Ed Sheeran.
