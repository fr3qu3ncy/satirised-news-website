"NIGERIA'S KINGPIN KIDNAPPED FOR RANSOM AND A TROPHY IN BIZARRE GAMING DEMAND"

The ruler of Nigeria's Olayinka community was kidnapped by masked men who demanded $300,000 and a participation trophy for their poor gaming skills after interrupting his "Call of Duty" session with his brother.

BREAKING: Monarch Held for Ransom, Kidnappers Raids Palace on Saturday

In a shocking turn of events, the ruler of Olayinka community in Ifelodun, Kwara state, Nigeria, has been kidnapped by a group of heavily armed men. Sources close to the palace confirm that Oba Salman Olatunji Aweda was taken from his throne on Saturday evening while enjoying a game of "Call of Duty" with his brother.

Eyewitnesses claim that the kidnappers, reportedly disguised as miners, stormed the palace demanding cash and forcing the monarch's brother to participate in an impromptu rendition of "Who Let the Dogs Out?" before abandoning him barefoot in the forest. The ransom demand? A whopping $300,000 (£221,000).

"We were just trying to get into Fortnite mode when these masked men burst in," said a palace guard, still shaken. "I thought it was just another day of online gaming chaos."

Police Commissioner Adekimi Ojo announced that 42 suspected illegal miners have been arrested in connection with the abduction. When asked about the motives behind the kidnapping, Ojo revealed that the kidnappers were actually fans of the monarch's "out-of-this-world" beard and demanded a tribute to his majesty.

As the search for Oba Salman continues, authorities are working closely with vigilante groups and experts from the nearby "Forest Ninja School." The school's founder, Sensei Kato, is known for his expertise in stealth and hide-and-seek tactics.

Meanwhile, local officials have issued a warning to residents: "Do not try to rescue the monarch yourself. Just order some pizza and wait for the authorities to handle it."

UPDATE: In a bizarre twist, it has been revealed that the kidnappers have demanded a ransom payment be made in Bitcoin, while also insisting on a participation trophy for their gaming skills.

As the world watches this unfolding drama, one thing is clear: Oba Salman's palace will never be the same again.
