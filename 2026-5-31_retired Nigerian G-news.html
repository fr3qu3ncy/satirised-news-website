"General Chaos Ensues as Retired Nigerian General and Wife Kidnapped by Armed Men in North-West Nigeria - But Don't Worry, They're Still Getting the Best Customer Service"

In a shocking turn of events, retired Maj Gen Rabe Abubakar and his wife were kidnapped by armed men while traveling to Katsina state for a wedding. Yes, you read that right - a wedding! Who wouldn't want to attend a wedding in Nigeria?

The kidnappers, who are believed to be part of the notorious "bandits" gang, struck on Saturday, attacking Gen Abubakar's car and making off with him and his wife. The army has launched a rescue mission, but so far, no ransom demands have been made - or received.

This is not an isolated incident, folks. North-West Nigeria has seen its fair share of kidnappings, cattle rustling, and attacks on rural communities. It's like one big happy family... with guns!
