MADONNA OFFERS REWARD FOR RETURN OF MISSING COACHELLA COSTUME... BUT ONLY IF YOU AGREE TO DANCE THE MACARONI DANCE

In a shocking turn of events, the Queen of Pop, Madonna, has offered a reward for the safe return of her missing Coachella costume, but only if you agree to perform the Macarena in front of a live audience. Yes, you read that right - the Macarena.

The outfits in question include the purple jacket, corset, and dress she wore during her surprise guest appearance with Sabrina Carpenter at the California event on Friday night. "These aren't just clothes," Madonna wrote on Instagram, "they are part of my history... and also a great excuse to bust out the ol' disco moves."

Madonna's vintage costume pieces went missing following the festival, and she is now offering a reward for their safe return - but only if you agree to perform the Macarena in front of a live audience. Because, why not?

The outfits are particularly special to Madonna because she wore the same boots, corset, and jacket during her Coachella performance 20 years ago. "It's like a full-circle moment," she said, "very meaningful for me... and also a great excuse to dust off my platform shoes."

Several other items from Madonna's personal archive "from the same era" have also disappeared, she wrote. In a statement, the Indio Police Department said clothes and jewellery were last seen "on a golf cart" on the festival grounds at 01:30 local time on Saturday. They may have fallen off the cart on the way to being loaded onto a bus - or maybe they just got up and danced the Macarena.
