NETANYAHU ORDERS ARMY TO "VIGOROUSLY ATTACK" HEZBOLLAH... WITH A SIDE OF CHIPS AND ALE

In a shocking turn of events, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ordered his military to "vigorously attack" Hezbollah targets in Lebanon, just two days after a ceasefire was extended by three weeks. But what the press didn't report is that Netanyahu's directive came with an important caveat: "And can someone please bring me some chips? I'm starving over here."

Sources close to the Israeli PM confirmed that he made the announcement while simultaneously binge-watching Netflix and eating a whole pizza by himself.

The news comes as six people were killed in strikes on southern Lebanon, but Netanyahu was quick to point out that it was just "a minor setback" and that the real enemy is still the chip delivery guy who always seems to get lost on the way to his house.

Hezbollah, however, was not impressed with Netanyahu's bold move. In a statement, they vowed to "target Israeli army vehicles with an extra dose of spice" in retaliation for the attack on Yohmor al-Shaqeef. When asked what kind of spice, they simply said "the good stuff."

The fragile ceasefire agreement has been strained further by the cross-border exchanges between Israel and Lebanon, but officials are optimistic that it can be salvaged with a few more rounds of negotiations over a plate of falafel.

In other news, a journalist was killed in Israeli attacks on Wednesday, and an international press advocacy group is calling for all parties to allow members of the media to work freely and safely. When asked about this, Netanyahu simply shrugged and said "oh, don't worry about it, I'll just have my people call CNN and tell them what happened."

UPDATE: It has been reported that Netanyahu's order was actually a misunderstanding with his assistant, who was trying to get him to attack the local pizza parlor because they ran out of his favorite topping.
