BREAKING: Secret Service Investigates Reports of Gunfire Near White House, But Only If They Find a Good Sale on Donuts

Washington D.C. - In a shocking turn of events, the US Secret Service is investigating reports of gunfire near the White House, but only if they can find a good sale on donuts first.

According to eyewitnesses, officers responded to reports of gunfire in Lafayette Park just after midnight local time (04:00 GMT), which caused some confusion among the locals. "I was just trying to get a good spot to watch the sunrise when suddenly, I heard 'gunfire' and then 'oh no, not again,' said Jane Doe, a regular park-goer.

The Secret Service quickly sprang into action, conducting a search of the park and surrounding area for any signs of suspicious activity. However, they quickly realized that they had forgotten to pack their donut-sniffing dogs, which led to a brief delay in the investigation.

"We were just trying to follow procedure, but then we remembered that the donut shop on 5th street has a sale today," said Secret Service Spokesman Anthony Guglielmi. "I mean, who doesn't love a good deal on glazed goodness?"

President Donald Trump was reportedly in the area at the time of the incident and was seen fleeing the scene in his motorcade, but only because he heard that the coffee shop on 3rd street had just received a new shipment of organic, gluten-free coffee beans.

The investigation continues, with the Secret Service promising to get to the bottom of things... once they finish their donuts. "We'll be back to you soon, folks," said Guglielmi. "But for now, let's just say that we're taking this thing very seriously... after our snack break."

In related news, the White House has announced that it will be hosting a family Easter dinner on Sunday, with an added twist: the Easter bunny will be wearing a Secret Service issued bulletproof vest.

UPDATE

The investigation has now been put on hold due to a sudden influx of Pokémon Go players in the area. "We apologize for the delay," said Guglielmi. "But we have to make sure that our agents are not distracted by any digital creatures... or discounts on donuts."
