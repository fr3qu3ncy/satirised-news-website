French-born Beninese activist Kemi Seba has been arrested by South African police, but in a bizarre twist, he's wanted for being an activist, not for saving the world as a superhero.

"Wanted Activist Arrested, But Not for Being a Superhero"

In a shocking turn of events, South African police have arrested French-born Beninese activist Kemi Seba, but not for his impressive mustache. Seba, who is wanted in Benin on charges of "inciting rebellion," was allegedly attempting to flee to Europe through Zimbabwe while wearing a bright yellow jacket and holding a microphone.

According to eyewitnesses, Seba's son, also 18 years old, was with him at the time of the arrest and looked like he had just walked out of a Justin Bieber concert. The two were said to be traveling in a vehicle with a "No Fins" bumper sticker, which has raised suspicions that they might have been trying to escape from an aquarium.

Seba, who is known for opposing French influence in Africa and backing West Africa's military leaders, has been accused of spreading Russian propaganda. However, sources close to the investigation say that he was actually just trying to get away from his nagging wife, who had been playing "I Will Survive" on repeat for weeks.

The police added that Seba is also a wanted fugitive in France and Benin for criminal activities relating to crimes against the state. However, they failed to mention that he was also wanted for being the worst DJ at any wedding in the past five years.

Seba's supporters have denounced the arrest as politically motivated and are planning a protest in front of the South African embassy in Paris, where they will demand that Seba be released and given a permanent residence permit. They argue that Seba is not just an activist, but also a great cook and has made the best jollof rice in all of West Africa.

"The Real Reason Seba Was Arrested"

According to insiders, Seba was actually arrested for trying to bring a bunch of contraband into the country. But not just any contraband – we're talking about a large quantity of knock-off Nike shoes and a pair of used socks that had been worn by the great Nelson Mandela himself.
