FOREIGN TOURISTS ARE FALLING OUT OF LOVE WITH GOA - BUT WHY?

In a shocking turn of events, foreign visitors to Goa have nearly halved from their pre-Covid peak. Yes, you read that right - they're not just dropping like flies, they're practically plummeting! It's like the whole world has gone crazy and no one wants to visit this tiny coastal state anymore.

But wait, it gets worse. The reason behind this sudden decline is not because of a lack of good beaches (although, let's be real, they are getting a bit too crowded with all those locals). Nope! It's because of a bunch of other totally unrelated reasons like:

* Money, money, money! Flights are so expensive now that it's like trying to buy a private island in the Maldives. Europeans and Russians are just not willing to shell out that kind of cash.
* Longer and more cumbersome visa procedures? Who needs that hassle when you can just stay home and binge-watch Netflix?
* And let's not forget about the visa fees - five years, who's got time for that?! It's like they're trying to discourage tourists from visiting!
* Oh, and did we mention the axing of a direct London Gatwick-Goa Air India service? That was just the cherry on top (or should I say, the coconut in the Goan beach shack).

But it gets even better! Local unions are now more powerful than ever and have taken to confronting taxi drivers who dare to use app-based services. It's like living in a scene from "The Hunger Games" - you can't even get a taxi without facing off against some angry locals.

And if all that wasn't enough, the government has also decided to ramp up its efforts to keep the beaches litter-free... which is great and all, but what about the garbage on the roads leading up to them? It's like they're trying to drive away tourists with a big ol' sign that says "Welcome to Goa - leave your sanity at the border".

But don't worry, Goa is not giving up yet! They're still trying to figure out why foreigners are abandoning ship and heading elsewhere in Asia. Maybe it has something to do with Vietnam and Sri Lanka being more affordable (hello, last-minute trips!) or the fact that they have easier on-arrival visas (bye-bye, tedious paperwork!).

So, if you ever find yourself planning a trip to Goa, just remember: it's not all sunshine and palm trees anymore. In fact, it's more like... well, try this: imagine a beachside shantytown with trash-filled roads and angry locals waiting for you at every turn. Yay!
