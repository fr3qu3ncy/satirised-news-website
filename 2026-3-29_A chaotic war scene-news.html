TRUMP'S WARRING WOES: THE GUT-INSTINCT APPROACH ISN'T CUTTING IT

Ah, the eternal conundrum of Donald Trump: how to wage war without actually planning for it. It's like trying to build a house with a deck of cards as blueprints – it might look good on paper, but when reality sets in, things get... messy.

As we mark one month since the conflict in Iran escalated, Trump's gut-instinct approach is proving to be less effective than a plate of lukewarm nachos at a Super Bowl party. His reliance on "feelings" and an inner circle of yes-men (and women) has left him wondering why his plans aren't quite... sticking.

It all started when Trump, in a moment of sheer brilliance, decided to bomb Iran's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei – only to find out that the regime was still standing, still fighting back, and still not exactly thrilled about being bombed by the Yanks. Who knew?

Now, we're not saying Trump didn't learn from his predecessors (okay, fine, we are). Dwight D. Eisenhower, the G.I. General himself, once said, "Plans are worthless, but planning is everything." But Trump's like, "Nah, I got this, guys!" And by "got this," we mean he got a bunch of civilians killed, including 1,464 Iranians (according to HRANA, those folks who monitor human rights stuff).

And let's not forget the pièce de résistance: when asked by Fox News Radio how long the war would last, Trump replied that it wouldn't be long – as in, when he "felt" it in his bones. Yeah, because nothing says "strategic thinking" like a guy who relies on his gut to determine the fate of nations.

Meanwhile, back in Tehran, the regime is all like, "Hey, we're still here, and we're not going anywhere!" It's like they have some sort of... plan (gasp!). And what do you know? They're even using those fancy-pants institutional things called "institutions" to stay afloat.

So, in conclusion, Trump's war strategy can be summed up as follows: 1) Bomb Iran; 2) Wait for people to rise up against the regime; 3) Realize that no one wants to die or get bombed; and 4) Go back to step 1 because... um, gut instincts!

As the great philosopher (and sometime-Dictator-for-a-Day), Mike Tyson, once said, "Everyone has a plan until they get hit." And boy, did Iran get hit – by Trump's incompetence.
