But wait, there's more! As part of their victory celebrations, One Nation supporters were seen dancing on top of the Sydney Harbour Bridge while dressed as giant chickens. Yes, you read that right - CHICKENS.

When asked for comment, Pauline Hanson simply shrugged and said, "Hey, we're a party of the people, by the people, and for the chickens."

The Liberal Party, which had previously held the seat, was quick to congratulate One Nation on their win. But then they realized they'd left their briefcase containing all their notes and strategies in the cafeteria, and now it's gone missing.

"Ah, no worries," said Angus Taylor, leader of the Liberal Party. "We'll just have to wing it from now on. After all, that's what One Nation does best, right?"

As for Michelle Milthorpe, the independent candidate who came in second, she was spotted sipping a cosmopolitan at a trendy cafe in Surry Hills and muttering, "Well, I suppose it could've been worse... but only slightly."

And so, with their newfound power comes great responsibility - or not. After all, One Nation has never actually governed anything before. This is going to be FUN.
