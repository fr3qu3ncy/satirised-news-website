BREAKING: Pope Goes Rogue on Africans, Tells Elon Musk to Sort Out His Space X Problems

Yaoundé, Cameroon - In a shocking turn of events, the Pontiff has descended upon Cameroon with an arsenal of scathing remarks that would make even the most seasoned heckler blush. Addressing a sea of adoring crowds at Saint Joseph's Cathedral in Bamenda, Pope Leo XIV unleashed a verbal lashing on foreigners who exploit Africa for profit.

"I'm not talking about just anyone, I'm talking about those pesky space cadets from Space X," the Pontiff declared to thunderous applause. "Elon Musk, get your act together and sort out your Mars colonization plan! We can't have you coming here, stealing our natural resources, and then complaining that the Wi-Fi on Mars is slow."

The crowd went wild as the Pope continued his tirade against corrupt government officials and separatist fighters. "You know who the real rebels are? The ones who refuse to wear matching socks with their camo uniforms! Come on, guys, get it together!"

Meanwhile, President Paul Biya looked like he'd just sucked on a lemon, muttering something about "idolatrous thirst for profit" and "hearts set free from an idolatrous thirst". The state television team responsible for live-streaming the event was overheard frantically whispering to each other, "Did we miss that part? Did he say 'idolatrous' or just 'adorable'?"

But the real kicker came when the Pope addressed the Anglophone conflict in Cameroon's two English-speaking regions. In a dramatic flourish, he turned to the crowd and proclaimed, "I'm not going to sugarcoat it, folks! This is all about resources, power struggles, and who gets to wear the coolest camo outfit!"

As the crowd roared with laughter and applause, a nun in the front row stood up and shouted, "Ooh, I've got one! Why did the separatist fighters refuse to play soccer with us? Because they were always trying to 'goal'-ize our village!"

The Pope smiled mischievously and quipped, "Well played, my dear. Well played."

It's unclear what sparked this uncharacteristically cheeky behavior from the usually staid Pontiff, but one thing is certain: no one will ever look at Africa, or Space X, in the same way again.

UPDATE: The Vatican has released a statement clarifying that the Pope's remarks were "totally taken out of context" and "just a joke". However, sources close to the event confirm that Elon Musk was overheard muttering something about "never underestimating the power of a well-placed insult".
