YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT HAPPENED TO THE ARTEMIS II CREW IN SPACE...

The astronauts on the Artemis II mission got lost in space and ended up at a rave party hosted by aliens in neon spandex, where they were forced to learn how to do the cha cha slide in zero gravity.

"MOONSHINE AND MARSUPIALS": ARTEMIS II CREW GETS LOST IN SPACE


In a bizarre turn of events, the astronauts on the Artemis II mission have gone awry. Instead of circling the Moon, they seem to have stumbled upon a rave party hosted by aliens in neon spandex.


"We were just trying to get some space selfies when suddenly, David Hasselhoff appeared out of nowhere and started singing 'Looking for Freedom' while riding a unicorn," said astronaut Reid Wiseman. "We thought it was part of the mission, but then things got weird."


The crew, consisting of Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch, and Jeremy Hansen, found themselves surrounded by Martians in sparkly jumpsuits, all grooving to techno music.


"It was like we entered an alternate dimension or something," said Koch. "We tried to take a selfie with the aliens, but they just started doing the floss and wouldn't stop."


As of 23:00 BST on Saturday, Nasa's online dashboard showed the Artemis II spacecraft had somehow ended up in the vicinity of a giant space disco ball, more than 180,000 miles from Earth.


"We're not sure how we got here or what kind of funk this is," said Hansen. "All we know is that we've been stuck in this lunar loop for three days and have to get back to Earth before our astronaut union contract runs out."


Meanwhile, back on Earth, NASA officials are scratching their heads, trying to figure out how the astronauts got lost in space.


"We're working with our top experts – the guys who created Pokémon Go – to try and track down the Artemis II crew," said a spokesperson for Nasa. "We'll get them back safely, but not before they learn how to do the cha cha slide in zero gravity."
