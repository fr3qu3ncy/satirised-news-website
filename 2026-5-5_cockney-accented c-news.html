In a bizarre turn of events, nine coal miners in Colombia died in an explosion caused by a gas build-up that was so bad, it's being called a "gas-astrophe", and their fellow workers were just trying to figure out who ate the last donut.

Breaking News: Coal Miners' Strike it Rich with Cockney Accent

In a shocking turn of events, nine coal miners in Colombia have died after an explosion at a mine in Sutatausa. But that's not the only surprise – the blast was caused by a build-up of gases, which is just a fancy way of saying "it was a real gas-astrophe!"

As emergency workers rushed to the scene, they found six miners still alive and kicking... or should I say, still singing in harmony? Yes, it turns out these coal miners were not only skilled at extracting coal from the earth but also had hidden talents as a cappella group. Who knew?

The captain of the regional fire department, Álvaro Farfán, was overheard saying, "We're still trying to rescue the miners' bodies, but first, we need to find out who ate the last donut in the break room." When asked about the explosion, he simply shrugged and said, "Oh, just another day at the coal mine... I mean, office."

Meanwhile, relatives of the victims rushed to the scene, their faces stained with coal dust and their black baseball caps sporting a white cockerel embroidered on top. One of them was overheard saying, "Blimey! Looks like they got a right old blast of gas!" Another relative chimed in, "Cheerio, mate! I hope they get the miners' bodies out soon – we need to get back to our tea and crumpets!"

As for the mine's operators, they're still trying to figure out how this happened. In a statement, they said, "We warned them about the potentially dangerous gas build-up after an inspection less than a month ago... but I suppose that's just a bit of old-fashioned coal miner humor." Who knew coal miners were such comedians?

In related news, Elton John has announced he'll be performing a charity concert to raise funds for the miners' families. When asked about his decision, he said, "I'm feeling a little 'Rocket Man'-sized sympathy for these poor souls... and also because I heard there's going to be free coal dust facial treatments at the event!"
