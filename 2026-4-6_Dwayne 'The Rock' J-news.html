BREAKING: Nigerian Army Foils Easter Egg Heist, Rescues 31 Hostages from Churched-out Bandits

In a daring operation, the Nigerian army has successfully foiled an Easter egg heist by a group of churched-out bandits who had taken 31 civilians hostage in Ariko, Kaduna state. The five-egg omelette masterminds behind the heist were reportedly armed to the teeth with AK-47s and an endless supply of church hymns.

Eyewitnesses claim that the army's swift response was like a hot knife through butter, leaving the bandits fleeing for their lives while leaving the hostages and the bodies of the five dead civilians (who were allegedly not as egg-cellent in their Easter egg hunt) behind. The Nigerian military has urged residents to come forward with any information that might help them track down the "fleeing terrorists" who were seen making a beeline for the nearest McDonald's.

In a bizarre twist, sources close to the investigation have revealed that the bandits were not your average, everyday crooks. They were allegedly led by none other than Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson, who was in Nigeria filming an action movie and decided to take his Method acting skills to new heights.

When asked for comment, The Rock's spokesperson said, "Dwayne was just trying to get into character, but things got a little out of hand. He's really sorry about the whole egg thing."

Meanwhile, the Nigerian government has announced plans to establish an Easter Egg Commission to investigate the incident and ensure that such atrocities never happen again.

In related news, the Ariko church has cancelled its Easter egg hunt due to "unforeseen circumstances."
