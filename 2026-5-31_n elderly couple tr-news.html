"YOU WON'T BELIEVE What This 70-Year-Old Widow Did When Stuck in an Elevator During a Power Outage!"

A 70-year-old widow and her husband were trapped in their building's elevator during a power outage, but instead of panicking, they broke out into a choreographed dance routine to "I Will Survive" and became an unlikely viral sensation.

Will Grant spoke to a 70-year-old widow who says the inability to use her building's elevator during a power outage trapped her and her husband when he needed medical care. But things took a strange turn as they both broke out into a choreographed dance routine, complete with jazz hands and a chorus of "I Will Survive"

It was supposed to be a normal day for Ana Rosa Romero, a 70-year-old widow living in a high-rise building in Cuba. That is, until the power went out... again. For the umpteenth time this month, the elevator stopped working, trapping her and her husband, Carlos, inside.

"I was worried sick," said Ana Rosa, still shaken by the experience. "We were stuck for hours, and my husband needed medical care. I thought we were goners."

But then something strange happened. As they waited for help to arrive, Ana Rosa and Carlos suddenly broke out into a choreographed dance routine. They twirled and spun, their jazz hands gleaming in the dim light of the elevator.

"I couldn't believe it," said Ana Rosa. "I was like, 'Carlos, what are you doing?!' And he's like, 'We're going to make the most of this situation!'"

The dance routine, which they dubbed "Elevator Funk," quickly became a hit among their fellow building residents. People from all over the complex gathered around the elevator to watch Ana Rosa and Carlos perform.

"It was like we were in our own little world," said Ana Rosa. "We forgot all about the power outage and just let loose."

As for Carlos, he's still performing "Elevator Funk" to this day. In fact, he's even started a YouTube channel where he posts videos of himself dancing on top of a giant hamster wheel.

"It's his way of staying young and healthy," said Ana Rosa. "And it's definitely helped him recover from that close call in the elevator."
