You Won't Believe What D4vd Did on His "Toughest" Day Ever... (Hint: It Involves Murder)

D4vd, a 21-year-old singer, pleaded not guilty to charges of first-degree murder after allegedly killing a 14-year-old girl and now faces three separate charges, including murder with special circumstances and murdering a witness.

BREAKING: D4vd, the Singer Who Was Clearly Having a Bad Day (and a Worse Week), Pleads Not Guilty to Murdering a Teenage Girl

In a shocking turn of events, David Anthony Burke, aka D4vd, the 21-year-old singer who was totally not having a midlife crisis, pleaded not guilty to charges of first-degree murder in the death of 14-year-old Celeste Rivas Hernandez. Because, you know, killing someone is just a Tuesday for some people.

According to eyewitnesses (okay, fine, it was just his lawyers), D4vd's defense team claimed that the "actual evidence will show that David did not kill Celeste" and that he was simply a victim of circumstance... or maybe just a really bad Uber driver. His attorneys also mentioned that they believed he was innocent, but only if we count all the times he said "I didn't do it" as actual proof.

The Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office, led by Nathan Hochman (who looks like he could use a good night's sleep), announced three sets of charges against D4vd. The first is for first-degree murder with special circumstances of "lying in wait," which is just code for "he was really sneaky." The second is for alleged murder for "financial gain," because who needs a music career when you can just kill people to get ahead? And the third charge is for murdering a witness to an investigation, which is basically just a fancy way of saying "he killed someone who knew too much."

When questioned about the long delay in making an arrest (seven whole months!), LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell responded with the kind of non-answer that makes you go "huh?" by saying, "My duty is not to fuel speculation. It's to deliver justice... and also maybe get some more coffee." He also mentioned that conditions of the teen's remains delayed determining a cause of death, but we're pretty sure he just meant that they ran out of donuts in the crime lab.

D4vd had remained in custody without bail since his arrest on Thursday, which is basically just code for "he's been stuck in a holding cell with a bunch of other people who have also committed crimes... and are now plotting against him." The singer was best known for moody, indie-pop hits like Here With Me and Romantic Homicide (which we're pretty sure is just code for "murder music"), but now he's probably going to be known for being a total murderer. Yikes.
