YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT HAPPENED WHEN A BUS DRIVER DANCED ON TOP OF HIS VEHICLE AND THE ELEPHANTS STARTED TRUMPETING!

A bus driver's impromptu cha-cha-slide on top of his vehicle, which was stopped by a giant plate of pad thai on the tracks, led to a catastrophic collision with a freight train and surrounding chaos, including trumpeting elephants.

BREAKING: Buses, Trains, and Elephants Collide in Bangkok; Chaos Ensues

In a bizarre incident that has left authorities scratching their heads, a freight train crashed into a public bus, engulfing it in flames and killing at least eight people. But wait, there's more! The bus was reportedly stopped on the tracks because its driver was busy eating a giant plate of pad thai. Yes, you read that right.

Eyewitnesses claim that as the train approached, the bus driver suddenly jumped out and started doing the cha-cha-slide on top of the vehicle. "I was just walking to the nearby 7-Eleven when I saw this guy dancing on the bus," said one witness. "I thought it was some kind of performance art, but then the train hit it and everything went up in flames."

The Deputy Transport Minister, Siripong Angkasakulkiat, blamed the driver for the collision. "Clearly, he was too busy enjoying his snack to pay attention to the oncoming train," said the minister.

Meanwhile, nearby elephants, who were enjoying a picnic in the area, became startled by the commotion and began trumpeting loudly. The authorities quickly called in the elephant whisperer from Thailand's Ministry of Agriculture and Cooperatives to calm them down.

The fire department arrived promptly, but not before the blaze had engulfed several nearby vehicles, including a Toyota Prius that was carrying a load of fresh coconuts.

"It was like something out of a movie," said one firefighter. "I mean, who expects to fight a bus fire with elephants trumpeting in the background?"

As the investigation continues, authorities are trying to determine what exactly caused the driver to abandon his bus and dance on top of it. One theory is that he had eaten too much spicy Thai food and was experiencing a sudden case of "Bangkok brain freeze."

In related news, the city's mayor has announced plans to install a new traffic light system that includes a special "elephant alarm" feature.

UPDATE:

* The bus driver has been charged with reckless endangerment and dancing on a moving vehicle.
* The elephants have been granted immunity from prosecution due to their "Good Samaritan" status in calming down the situation.
* The 7-Eleven store nearby has reported a surge in pad thai sales.
