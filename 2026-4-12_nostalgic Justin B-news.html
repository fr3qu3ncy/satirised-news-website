Justin Bieber Headlines Coachella with Nostalgia-Fueled Set, But It's Not Just His Music That's Old

In a move that left fans scratching their heads and wondering if they had traveled back in time to the early 2000s, Justin Bieber took to the stage at Coachella last night and spent most of his gig singing along to YouTube videos of his old hits like "Baby" and "Never Say Never." But it wasn't just his music that was showing its age – Bieber's fashion sense also seemed stuck in a time warp. The 32-year-old singer took the stage dressed in a hoodie and shorts, making him look like he had just rolled out of bed and stumbled onto the stage.

As he performed, Bieber repeatedly asked the crowd how far back they went, clearly trying to gauge their level of nostalgia for his old music. And it seemed like most of them were right there with him – rocking out to songs that are older than some of their own kids. It was a true blast from the past, and one that had everyone in attendance singing along and reminiscing about the good old days.

But things took a turn for the absurd when Bieber started pulling up clips of himself on YouTube, including the infamous video where he confronted a photographer and said "It's not clocking to you, I'm standing on business." The crowd went wild, but we're pretty sure it was just because they were confused – did someone say something about a bus?

And if all that wasn't enough, Bieber also brought out his wife Hailey, who looked like she had just stepped off the cover of a 2005 teen magazine. She seemed to be having the time of her life, dancing along to every song and even taking a selfie with Tems, who was performing as one of Bieber's musical guests.

Meanwhile, other celebrities in attendance included Timothée Chalamet and Kylie Jenner, who were both spotted looking like they had just rolled out of a 2002 Myspace page. We're not sure what's more astonishing – the fact that Bieber is still relevant after all these years or the fact that Timmy and Kylie are still trying to make it in Hollywood.

All in all, it was a night to remember at Coachella, with Bieber's nostalgia-fueled set bringing back memories of a bygone era. And who knows? Maybe next year he'll even bring back MySpace itself – because if there's one thing we can count on, it's that Bieber will always be ahead of the curve... or behind it, depending on how you look at it.
