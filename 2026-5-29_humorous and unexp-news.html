BREAKING: Blue Origin Rocket Explodes, But Don't Worry, It's Just a Minor Anomaly... Said No One Ever

In a shocking turn of events, a Blue Origin New Glenn rocket exploded on a launch pad at Cape Canaveral in Florida on Thursday night. Because what's more surprising than a rocket just suddenly exploding? I mean, it's not like they're made of unstable, flammable materials or anything.

According to eyewitnesses (okay, fine, it was just the guy who forgot his lunch in the break room), the explosion occurred during a "hotfire test," which is apparently what Blue Origin calls their version of "let's see if this thing still works." The company's spokesperson, who was conveniently dressed as Batman for some reason, said there were no injuries from the incident. "All personnel have been accounted for... and also our lunch budget. We'll just have to order pizza again."

The explosion was described as an "anomaly," which is just a fancy way of saying "we have no idea what happened." But don't worry, folks! Blue Origin has promised to provide updates as they learn more... or in other words, when someone finally figures out how to use Google.

Meanwhile, Jeff Bezos, the billionaire founder of Amazon and also the guy who thought it'd be a great idea to start a space company, was seen fleeing the scene on a unicycle while playing "Who Let the Dogs Out" on his harmonica. When asked for comment, he simply said, "Well, that's just peachy."

In related news, NASA has announced plans to launch a new program to teach astronauts how to properly handle exploding rockets... or at least, how to do it without anyone getting hurt. Because priorities.

UPDATE: Blue Origin has since released a statement saying the explosion was caused by a rogue gang of space squirrels who were attempting to hijack the rocket for their own nefarious purposes. When asked for comment, the space squirrels replied, "Hey, we're just trying to get in on the whole space thing. You know, make some nuts... I mean, money."
