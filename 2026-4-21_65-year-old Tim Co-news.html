Apple's Future Will Be a Boring, iPhone-Fueled Snooze Fest Under New CEO John Ternus

Apple's future under new CEO John Ternus is predicted to be "exciting" in the sense that it will be boring and uneventful, much like Tim Cook's tenure before him.

HOW WILL APPLE CHANGE UNDER 'PRODUCT GUY' JOHN TERNUS?

Oh boy, are you ready for this? Apple's future is about to get a whole lot more... exciting. And by exciting, I mean boring.

Let's face it, folks, Apple has been planning this succession for a while now. Rumors were circulating that 65-year-old Tim Cook was thinking of stepping down (aka taking a long nap) and handing over the reins to someone who doesn't have a Twitter account with more followers than his company has employees. And that someone is... John Ternus, aka "The Guy Who Won't Rock The Boat"!

I had the chance to meet him in the UK recently, and let me tell you, he's as exciting as a lecture on crop rotation at an agricultural conference. I asked him if he was the heir apparent, and he gave me that classic politician answer: "I'm just here for the free coffee and Wi-Fi." Okay, not really, but it felt like it!

Now, Apple faces some serious challenges, folks! How Ternus responds to them will go a long way in deciding the tech giant's future. Or should I say, its ability to sell more iPhones?

HARDWARE FOCUS: THE PRODUCT GUY

You could say Apple chose a replacement for Cook who is basically a younger, less cranky version of him. Calm, steady, reliable - just like your accountant at tax time! While co-founder Steve Jobs was the kind of guy who'd throw a tantrum because his Macbook wasn't charging fast enough (true story), Tim Cook and John Ternus are more like... well, accountants.

And don't even get me started on women in tech. Apple is still waiting to appoint its first female CEO. Maybe they're just saving up for a bigger celebration party than the one they had when Steve Jobs came back from that whole "dying" thing (just kidding, Tim!).

NEW BOSS, NEW CHALLENGES: AI AND ROBOTS

The big challenge for Ternus is... wait for it... Artificial Intelligence! Because what Apple needs right now is more software engineers with a passion for coffee and less of those pesky robots taking over the world. Yeah, that's exactly what we need - more iPhones and slightly-less-boring gadgets!

And then there's AI itself, which is basically just a fancy word for " computers being too smart for their own good." Apple is still trying to figure out how to make it not suck (in the words of our friend, Steve Jobs). Will Ternus succeed where Cook failed? Only time will tell... and by succeed, I mean not completely screw up the company.

THE DIPLOMATIC CHALLENGE

There's also the tiny matter of getting along with a US president who is notoriously fickle (aka Trump). Just kidding, folks! Tim Cook was all about being neutral and donating to Trump's inauguration fund. That's just the kind of guy he is - all about giving gifts to politicians and making sure Apple stays in their good books.

In conclusion, John Ternus is basically the tech equivalent of a warm hug on a cold winter morning. And by "warm hug," I mean a sedative to put you right to sleep. But hey, at least Apple will still sell iPhones!
